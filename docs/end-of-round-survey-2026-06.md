# PowerUp — End-of-Round Survey (2026-06)

Anonymous, ~3 minutes. Distribute as a Google Form (no login required so it
stays anonymous) posted once in #powerup, with a follow-up DM nudge from the
Slack bot two days later to anyone who hasn't visited the form URL.

## Intro copy

> Thanks for being part of PowerUp. Ten quick questions to help us shape the
> next round. No names, no logins — answer as bluntly as you like.

## Questions

**3. How would you describe your own participation this round?**

**10. One thing we should change, add, or drop for next round?** *(open text — even one sentence helps)*

## Design notes

- Anonymous, no Google login required, no IP capture in Forms settings.
- Outcome questions (Q1–Q2) lead before the engagement-level segmenter (Q3) so
  disengaged respondents don't feel sheepish before they answer.
- Q5 ("most enjoyed") and Q9 ("screens used") are deliberately distinct: one
  measures emotional resonance, the other measures surface coverage. A
  respondent who didn't enjoy the leaderboards but still checked them gives
  useful product signal.
- Shop split into "bought / looked but didn't buy" rather than yes/no — the gap
  between aware-but-didn't-spend and never-visited is the more actionable one.
- Q9 omits the Challenge page on purpose: Photo Bingo is already covered by Q5
  under "what you enjoyed most" — listing the page separately would
  double-count.
